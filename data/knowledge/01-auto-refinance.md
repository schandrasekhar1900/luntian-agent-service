# Auto loan refinancing (Luntian Bank)
Existing Luntian auto loan customers with no missed payments in the last 12 months can refinance the outstanding balance to lower their monthly amortization. Refinance rate: 7.88% p.a. (diminishing balance), terms of 12 to 60 months counted from the refinance date. Extending the term lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan. Processing fee: PHP 5,000, added to the loan or paid upfront. The vehicle stays as collateral under the existing chattel mortgage. Approval takes 2 to 3 banking days after documents are complete. Refinancing replaces the old loan, so any payment holiday on the old auto loan is not available at the same time.
