# Home loan payment holiday (Luntian Bank)
Home loan customers with at least 12 consecutive months of on-time payments may request a payment holiday of 1 to 3 months for a life event: childbirth or parental leave, job change, medical recovery, or a temporary drop in household income. During the holiday no amortization is collected, but interest continues to accrue and is capitalised (added to the principal) at the end of the holiday. The loan is then re-amortized over the remaining term, so the monthly amortization after the holiday is slightly higher. Only one payment holiday is allowed per 24 months. A restructuring fee of PHP 5,000 applies. Requests must be submitted at least 10 banking days before the first paused due date.
