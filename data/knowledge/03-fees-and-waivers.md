# Restructuring fees and fee waivers (Luntian Bank)
Standard fees: auto refinance processing fee PHP 5,000; home loan payment holiday or restructuring fee PHP 5,000; pre-termination fee 3% of the prepaid principal for auto loans within the first 24 months, waived after 24 months. Fee waivers are never approved by the digital assistant. A waiver of the restructuring fee may be granted by a Loans Care officer for Preferred customers with a clean payment record who request relief because of a qualifying life event (for example maternity leave). The officer must document the reason and the customer's consent. Officers may waive up to PHP 5,000 per request; larger amounts need branch manager approval.
