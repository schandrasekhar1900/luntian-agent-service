# Support for life events (Luntian Bank)
Luntian Bank offers relief options for customers facing a temporary change in income because of a life event such as maternity or paternity leave, a new baby, a job change, or medical recovery. Options: (1) refinance the auto loan to a longer term for a lower monthly payment; (2) a 1 to 3 month home loan payment holiday; (3) a one-to-one restructuring plan with a Loans Care officer. Customers can combine options only with officer approval. Lia, the digital assistant, can explain options and prepare an application; a Loans Care officer completes approvals, exceptions and fee waivers.
